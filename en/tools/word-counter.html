How to use the word counter

Type directly into the box or paste text copied from a document, an email or a web page. The count updates as you type, with no button to press.

Your text never leaves your browser: all the counting happens on your own device, so you can use it with private content.

What this tool counts

  • Words: groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks.
  • Characters: every letter, number, symbol and space.
  • No spaces: characters excluding spaces and line breaks.
  • Sentences: fragments ending in a period, exclamation mark or question mark.
  • Paragraphs: blocks of text separated by a blank line.
  • Reading time: estimated at about 200 words per minute.

Frequently asked questions

How many words are on a page?

It depends on font size and margins, but a single-spaced page usually holds 400 to 500 words, and a double-spaced page 250 to 300.

Are spaces counted as characters?

The "Characters" result includes them. If you need the number without spaces, check "No spaces".

Is my text saved?

No. Your text is not sent to or stored on any server; it is processed only in your browser.
