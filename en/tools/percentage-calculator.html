How to calculate a percentage

A percentage is a part out of one hundred. Depending on what you want to find, there are three common calculations, and the calculator above handles all of them.

  • X% of a number: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. Example: 20% of 150 is 150 × 20 ÷ 100 = 30.
  • What percent one number is of another: divide the number by the total and multiply by 100. Example: 30 out of 120 is 30 ÷ 120 × 100 = 25%.
  • Percentage change: subtract the starting value from the final value, divide by the starting value and multiply by 100. Example: from 80 to 100 is (100 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = +25%.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a discounted price?

Work out the discount percentage of the price and subtract it. For example, for a $50 item with 20% off: 20% of 50 is 10, so the final price is $40.

How do I add a percentage to a number?

Work out the percentage of the number and add it. For example, to add 21% to 100: 21% of 100 is 21, so the total is 121.

If something rises 50% and then falls 50%, is it back to the start?

No. Start at 100, rise 50% and you reach 150, but a 50% drop from 150 leaves you at 75. Each percentage is calculated on the current value, not the original.

What does a negative percentage change mean?

The final value is lower than the starting one. A change of −25% means it dropped by a quarter.
